## 3. Core Focus: AI UGC Video Ads (Gig 3)

### The Problem It Solves:
Hiring physical UGC creators costs \$100–\$300+ per video and takes 1–2 weeks for shipping and filming. AI UGC ads allow dropshippers and DTC brand owners to get authentic-looking creator testimonials and product review ads in under 48 hours for a fraction of the cost.

### Packaging & Offer Structure:
- **Tier 1 (Starter Hook - $25 - $35)**:
  - 1x 15-second viral hook ad featuring an AI creator.
  - Subtitles, basic sound effects, vertical 9:16 format.
- **Tier 2 (Standard UGC Ad - $50 - $75)**:
  - 1x 30-second full review ad (Hook + Problem + Product demo + CTA).
  - AI spokesperson + client product footage b-roll + dynamic captions + trending audio.
- **Tier 3 (Viral Testing Bundle - $120 - $160)**:
  - 3x video variations with 3 different hooks & 2 different AI creators for A/B ad split testing.
- **Add-on Upsells**:
  - 24-hour express delivery (+\$20).
  - Ad script copywriting (+\$15).
  - Multiple aspect ratios (1:1 feed + 9:16 story/reels) (+\$15).
